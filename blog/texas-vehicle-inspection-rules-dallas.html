Do You Still Need a Car Inspection in Texas? Dallas Rules for 2026

Updated October 3, 2026  ·  By the CarNation Elite technicians  ·  3 min read

Need it looked at now? We offer vehicle and pre-purchase inspections in Dallas, with a 12-month / 12,000-mile warranty on every repair.

Since January 1, 2025, most personal vehicles in Texas no longer need an annual safety inspection. Instead, owners pay a $7.50 inspection program replacement fee when they renew their registration. But if your car is registered in Dallas County, it still needs an emissions inspection before you can renew.

Commercial vehicles still need a safety inspection. Here is what that means for Dallas drivers, based on the state's rule change and what we see at CarNation Elite.

Emissions testing is still required in Dallas County and eight surrounding counties: Tarrant, Collin, Denton, Ellis, Johnson, Kaufman, Parker and Rockwall. Check the Texas DMV or TCEQ websites for the latest details on your vehicle.

What the emissions test checks

For most cars built since 1996, the emissions test is an OBD-II computer check, not a tailpipe sniff. The inspector plugs into your car's diagnostic port and confirms:

  • The check engine light works and is not on
  • There are no stored emissions-related trouble codes
  • The car's self-tests ("readiness monitors") have completed
  • The gas cap seals properly (on vehicles that require that check)

The most common reasons Dallas cars fail

1. The check engine light is on

An illuminated check engine light is an automatic emissions failure, even if the car runs fine. Get the cause diagnosed and fixed before your test. See our guide to the 15 most common check engine light causes.

2. Readiness monitors not ready

If the battery was recently disconnected or codes were just cleared, the car's self-tests reset. It needs a mix of city and highway driving, often 50 to 100 miles over a few days, before the monitors are ready again. Clearing a code right before an inspection is a common way to fail.

3. A loose or worn gas cap

An EVAP leak from a bad gas cap seal can set a code and fail the test. It's the cheapest fix there is.

Safety still matters, even without the sticker

The state dropped the requirement, not the risk. Worn brakes, bald tires and burned-out brake lights are still dangerous on Dallas highways, and they're still illegal to drive with. We recommend a quick safety check once a year, especially before a road trip or a Texas summer:

  • Brake pad and rotor condition
  • Tire tread depth and sidewall cracking
  • All lights, wipers and horn
  • Windshield cracks in the driver's view
  • Battery health and cooling system hoses

Buying a used car? Get a pre-purchase inspection

Without annual safety inspections, there's less of a paper trail on a used car's condition. A pre-purchase inspection by an independent shop is the best way to know what you're buying. It covers far more than the old state inspection did: engine and transmission health, stored codes, suspension, leaks, and signs of accident or flood damage. Our used car buying checklist explains what to look for.

Frequently asked questions

Does Texas still require car inspections in 2026?

Personal vehicles no longer need the annual safety inspection, which ended January 1, 2025. Vehicles registered in Dallas County and eight surrounding counties still need an emissions inspection, and commercial vehicles still need a safety inspection.

Do I need an emissions test in Dallas County?

Yes. Dallas County is one of the North Texas counties where emissions testing is still required before registration renewal.

What is the Texas inspection program replacement fee?

A $7.50 fee added at registration renewal in place of the old safety inspection. Brand-new vehicles of the two most recent model years pay $16.75 once, covering two years.

Will my car fail emissions with the check engine light on?

Yes. An illuminated check engine light is an automatic failure. Have the cause diagnosed and repaired, then drive normally for a few days so the readiness monitors complete before testing.
